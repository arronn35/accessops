# Percevia AI Customer Lead Criteria

Updated: 2026-06-28

This file defines what counts as a real customer prospect for the 100-lead list.

## What "Real Customer" Means

A row is a real customer prospect when it is a real operating company that could reasonably buy, pilot, resell, or refer Percevia AI based on its public business model.

This does not mean the company is guaranteed to buy, invest, reply, or be interested. It means the company has an observable fit with Percevia AI and a publicly verifiable business contact route.

## Accepted Prospect Types

### Agency Prospects

Agencies are accepted when they sell at least one of:

- E-commerce implementation
- Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, Shopware, Webflow, Framer, React, or Next.js delivery
- UX/UI, digital product, or conversion optimization
- Accessibility, QA, compliance, or design-system services

Why they fit:

- They can buy the Agency plan.
- They can resell accessibility reports to multiple clients.
- They can use root-cause grouping and remediation boards as a client service.

### E-commerce Prospects

E-commerce companies are accepted when they have:

- A public storefront
- Product detail, listing, cart, account, or checkout flows
- EU/TR market exposure or multi-market online sales
- A public business, sales, support, or customer-care contact address

Why they fit:

- They have accessibility risk in conversion-critical flows.
- Product, cart, checkout, filter, variant, and form components often create repeated issues.
- They may benefit from recurring scans and executive/technical reports.

## Email Rules

Use only:

- Public business or role emails on the company website
- Public sales/partnership/contact emails
- Public support/customer-care emails only when no better business email is found, and mark them as support

Do not use:

- Guessed personal emails
- Private databases
- Personal emails from unrelated profiles
- `no-reply`, legal, privacy, DPO, HR, careers, abuse, webmaster, or postmaster inboxes

## Quality Notes

- Source URL is mandatory.
- Every row must have a personalization hook.
- Support/customer-care emails need manual review before sending.
- Do not run a substantial third-party scan before the company gives permission.
- Do not present any lead as "ready to buy"; use "qualified prospect" or "potential customer."
