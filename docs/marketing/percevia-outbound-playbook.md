# Percevia AI Outbound Playbook

Updated: 2026-06-27

## Positioning

Percevia AI is an accessibility operations platform for teams that need to find, explain, prioritize, remediate, monitor, and report accessibility issues without pretending that automated scans are legal certification.

Core message:

> No overlay. No legal promises. Real accessibility fixes, operationalized.

Primary value:

- Turn accessibility findings into a client-ready or team-ready remediation workflow.
- Group repeated issues by root cause so teams do not drown in duplicate scanner output.
- Produce branded reports and remediation roadmaps for agencies, e-commerce teams, and product teams.
- Keep AI and visual evidence privacy-controlled, opt-in, and review-first.

Avoid:

- "Guaranteed compliance"
- "ADA/WCAG/EAA certified"
- "One-click compliance"
- "AI fixes your site automatically"
- Legal threat-based messaging

## Initial Market

Primary geography: Turkey + European Union.

Primary ICP:

1. Web, Shopify, WooCommerce, Webflow, Framer, Next.js, and e-commerce agencies.
2. E-commerce brands selling into the EU or operating complex storefronts.

Secondary ICP:

- SaaS/product teams with frequent releases.
- Accessibility consultants who need repeatable reporting and remediation tracking.
- Freelancers and small studios that can resell accessibility review as a retainer.

## Segment Angles

### Agencies

Offer:

> Add accessibility reporting and remediation retainers to your client services without building the tooling yourself.

Best hooks:

- Branded client reports.
- Multi-client workflow.
- Root-cause grouping for repeated component issues.
- Remediation board for developer handoff.
- Clear disclaimers that protect agency credibility.

Preferred plan:

- Agency, EUR 129/month.

### E-commerce Brands

Offer:

> Catch product-page, checkout, mobile, form, and contrast issues before they cost customers or create avoidable compliance pressure.

Best hooks:

- Checkout and product detail pages.
- Mobile menu, modal, and form states.
- EU accessibility pressure around e-commerce services.
- Executive summary plus technical fixes.
- No overlay and no legal overpromising.

Preferred plan:

- Starter for small brands.
- Team for larger internal product/e-commerce teams.

## Lead Acceptance Rules

Accept a lead only when all of these are true:

- The website is public and belongs to the company being contacted.
- The contact email is visibly published on the company site or a trusted public directory.
- The source URL is recorded.
- The source context explains where the email was found.
- The outreach angle is specific enough to personalize the first sentence.

Preferred emails:

- `hello@`
- `info@`
- `contact@`
- `partnerships@`
- `agency@`
- `marketing@`
- `sales@`

Use cautiously:

- `support@` or customer-service inboxes, only when no better business address is public and the fit is strong.
- Named personal emails, only when explicitly published for business inquiries and not inferred or guessed.

Reject:

- `no-reply@`
- guessed personal emails
- leaked or private database emails
- emails copied from enrichment tools without a public source
- addresses on pages that prohibit marketing contact

## Fit Scoring

Use a 1-100 score. Prioritize leads above 70.

Agency scoring:

- Multi-client potential: 0-25
- Technical service fit: 0-20
- E-commerce or product focus: 0-20
- Public business email quality: 0-15
- Personalization hook quality: 0-10
- TR/EU market fit: 0-10

E-commerce scoring:

- EU sales or EU market signal: 0-20
- Storefront complexity: 0-20
- Checkout/product-page accessibility risk: 0-20
- Brand maturity: 0-15
- Public business email quality: 0-15
- Personalization hook quality: 0-10

## Campaign Design

First batch:

- 30 agency leads.
- 30 e-commerce leads.

Expansion target:

- 120 validated leads.
- After two weeks, expand the best-performing segment to 300 leads.

Cadence:

- Day 1: initial email.
- Day 4: follow-up with example report offer.
- Day 9: use-case follow-up.
- Day 15: polite close-out.

Volume:

- Start at 10-20 emails/day per sending mailbox.
- Stop or slow down if bounce rate exceeds 5%.
- Stop using any source pattern that produces unclear or low-quality contacts.

## Compliance Checklist

Before sending:

- SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are configured.
- The sender is a real person or clearly identified company mailbox.
- Plain text email is used.
- No attachment in cold email.
- Company identity and website are clear.
- Physical address or registered-company footer is included where required.
- Every email includes a simple opt-out sentence.
- Suppression list is respected.

Outreach language must include:

- A clear reason for contact.
- No legal compliance guarantee.
- No claim that Percevia AI replaces expert review.
- No third-party scan results unless the recipient gave permission to run the scan.

## Success Metrics

Lead quality:

- 100% source URL coverage.
- 90%+ public role/business emails.
- Less than 5% hard bounce.

Campaign response:

- 40%+ open rate if tracked.
- 3-8% positive reply target.
- 1-3% meeting/demo target for the first test batch.

Pipeline:

- Agency replies about white-label reports.
- E-commerce replies about checkout/product accessibility.
- Requests for sample reports.
- Requests for pricing or onboarding.
